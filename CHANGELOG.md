# Changelog

What changed in each release, in plain language, for the people using the app rather than the people building it.

This project uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org). Release notes are derived from the issues in each milestone.

## 1.1.0

Four things found by using version 1.0, and a tidying pass.

Every card on Today can be taken off now, including the one at the top. That card was never really the top card: two rows the app keeps and never draws were sitting above it, so the protection meant for the lead slot was landing on whatever card happened to be first on screen.

Each choice in the capture menu shows in its own color, the way the rest of the app colors things.

Memos has the help text it was missing. Five more screens gained a help button that had never been drawn, so the text written for them was finally reachable: care team, care threads, money, chapters and medications.

What you took out is now called Deleted Items, and anything waiting there can be deleted for good, behind a confirmation that says plainly it cannot be undone.

Settings is grouped so you can guess where a thing lives, and the support link the app always meant to offer at the bottom of it is there.

## 1.0.0

The first release. A private care notebook for the family member who ends up holding the whole picture.

Keep one record of everything: calls, visits, appointments, medications, documents, bills, and the long processes like applications and appeals. Rough dates are fine, every field is optional, and nothing is filed for you. The trail is ordered by when things happened rather than when you found time to type them.

Everything stays on the phone. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads, no subscription. Your whole notebook exports as one encrypted file you hold yourself, and it restores on another phone.

Version one is in American English. Spanish, Chinese and Arabic are written and wait on a native speaker who understands the American care system.
