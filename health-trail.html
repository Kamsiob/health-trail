The idea

One trail, everything on it.

Providers rarely talk to each other, so in practice the family caregiver becomes the information hub. Health Trail gives that job some structure: one record of every call, visit, incident, medication change, document, and dollar, kept for years and searchable in seconds. It records, organizes, and counts. It never diagnoses, never reminds, and never tells you what a number means.

What it's for

What you'd actually use it for.

Good for
✓Logging a call in under fifteen seconds, from a hallway or a waiting room
✓Following one incident from the first report to the resolution, with names and dates
✓Handing an administrator, a lawyer, or a new doctor a record that actually holds together
✓Keeping track of bills, standing instructions, and the money that never quite lines up
Not for
✕Medication reminders or dosage alerts. It keeps the record, never the alarm.
✕Medical or legal advice of any kind
✕A shared live view for several people at once. One notebook, one point person.
✕Anything leaving your phone unless you choose to send it

Health Trail is built around one honest limit: it counts what happened and stops there. What a number means is always yours to judge.

How it's structured

One notebook, four ways to look at it.

Chapters

Every place: home, a hospital stay, a rehab facility, a nursing home. Each chapter keeps its own dates, incidents, documents, and care team, archived but never lost.

Care threads

Physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech, nursing, whatever is running in parallel. Each gets its own color and keeps its whole story even after it ends.

The trail

Every call, visit, and change, in order. Recent months stay open, older ones fold closed, and a year scrubber jumps you anywhere in between.

Everything connects

A medication knows its own incidents. A person knows every call involving them. Tap anything underlined and it takes you there. No dead ends.

✳
The engine

It counts. It never judges.

Every digest, month review, and pattern comes from a small deterministic engine, not a model. It queries your real entries, does the arithmetic in code, and writes the sentence from a template. "Three of the five resolved incidents involved the evening shift" is something it can say. What that means is always left to you.

No ranges, no normal values, no color-coded numbers, anywhere in the app.

Capture

It forgives a bad memory.

Every field is optional. A rough date like "sometime this week" is a valid answer. Anything you can't categorize goes into an Unfiled tray, where the app guesses a home and you confirm it with one tap. It never files anything on its own.

Stopping for months and coming back is normal. There is no streak to break and no catch-up screen waiting for you.

Questions

Common questions.

Is Health Trail free?
Yes, completely. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no feature held back behind a payment. Support is an optional donation, never a requirement.
Does Health Trail collect my data?
No. It does not collect, transmit, or sell any data. Everything you record stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics of any kind.
Is Health Trail a medical app?
No. It is a record-keeping app for family caregivers. It gives no medical or legal advice, and it never interprets a measurement, a count, or a symptom.
Can more than one family member use it at the same time?
Health Trail is built around a single point person rather than shared live editing. Anything in the notebook can be exported as a PDF or text and shared with other family members through whatever app they already use.
Does it remind me to give medications?
No, and this is deliberate. Health Trail keeps a record of medications and dose history. It never reminds, alerts, or sets an alarm for a dose.
What platforms will Health Trail run on?
Android first, built in Kotlin. A web version that keeps all data in the browser on your own device is planned to follow.
Is Health Trail available yet?
Not yet. It is fully designed and specified and is moving into development. This page exists ahead of release, the same honest way the rest of the app is built.
Is Health Trail open source?
Yes. The app will be licensed AGPLv3 once released. Its free 57-template starter catalog is separately licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and usable with or without the app.
What languages will it support?
English, Spanish, Chinese, and Arabic, all from the first release, including full right-to-left layout for Arabic.

Privacy Policy

This is the policy that will govern Health Trail once it ships. Published ahead of release so it's here before anyone needs it, and it will carry an effective date the day the app first goes live.

Health Trail is built so this policy can be short.

The short version: Health Trail does not collect your data. Not "collects but anonymizes," not "collects but doesn't sell." It does not collect it. Everything you write, every photo you take, and every document you save stays on your phone.

What happens on your device

Care team, medications, appointments, chapters, care threads, the trail, progress measurements, documents and their attachments, money, standing instructions, and settings are all processed and stored locally on your phone, in a database encrypted at rest. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. There are no servers, no accounts, and no analytics, so there is nowhere for your data to go even if we wanted it to.

When the app touches the internet

In the first release, Health Trail makes no network requests at all. It works fully offline, including in airplane mode, because there is nothing else for it to do.

If a future version adds an optional way to download additional templates or a facility pack, that will only happen when you choose to download one, over an ordinary request to GitHub, and this policy will be updated first to describe it plainly.

What Google sees

If you install Health Trail from Google Play, Google provides aggregate statistics like install counts, as it does for every app. That comes from Google's own systems, not from anything inside Health Trail, and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Backups

The backup file you export contains your whole notebook. It is created on your device and saved wherever you choose. It is encrypted by default with a passphrase you set. If that passphrase is lost, the file cannot be recovered. There is no backdoor and no way for us to help, because we never have a copy.

Children

Health Trail is not directed at children, and because it collects nothing, there is nothing to collect from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes. Given how the app is built, meaningful changes are unlikely.
