# The interface is rebuilt on Material 3 Expressive

The design authority. Where this and `DESIGN.md` disagree, this wins.

**The interface is replaced, not adjusted.** Google's Material 3 Expressive
components and assets are the foundation; this app's character is applied on
top of them. Every screen is written from the ground up against that
foundation. Nothing is retrofitted, restyled or partially converted.

**The back end does not change.** The repository, the schema, the change log,
the export container, the decryptor, the fixtures and every rule in
`contract/DATA-CONTRACT.md` stay exactly as they are. This is a user interface
replacement and nothing below the UI is in scope. A change that needs a schema
change is out of scope and goes to the owner.

---

## 1. The foundation, and what it took to get it

**The foundation is `androidx.compose.material3:material3:1.5.0-alpha26`**,
pinned past the bom in `libs.versions.toml`. It carries:

- `MaterialExpressiveTheme` and `ExperimentalMaterial3ExpressiveApi`
- `MotionScheme`, including the expressive motion scheme
- `ButtonGroup`, `ConnectedButtonGroup`, `SplitButton`, `FloatingToolbar`,
  `LoadingIndicator`
- `ShapeDefaults`, and `MaterialShapes`, the 35 shape library, **inside
  material3**, so no `androidx.graphics:graphics-shapes` is needed

**This section used to say all of that was already on the classpath in 1.4.0
and it was wrong.** In stable 1.4.0 the theme and the motion scheme are
`internal` and the expressive components do not exist. The classfiles are in
the aar, which is what the claim was read from, and Kotlin's `internal` does
not appear in a file listing. **The compiler is the only check that answers
this.** D179 has the measurements.

Everything else in Compose stays on `compose-bom 2026.08.00`, which pins UI
1.12.0 stable. **One artifact is off the stable channel and it is deliberate.**
Before any release build, check the pin against what is stable then.

## 2. What the language is

From Material 3 Expressive as Google ships it, and the four rounds of mockups
the owner approved in `docs/screenshots/m3v4-{0..5}-light.png`. **Open those
files; do not build from memory of them.**

- **Vibrant tonal containers on a quiet near-white canvas.** Color lives in
  the containers, never spread across the ground.
- **Shape variety.** The shape library is used for variety. One radius on
  every surface is the thing that makes an app read as undesigned.
- **Editorial type with hard weight contrast.** A screen leads with something.
- **Springs with visible overshoot**, and a capture control that morphs.
- **Short navigation bars.**
- **One saturated block per screen at most**, and everything else quiet. Six
  tinted things of equal weight is a rainbow and gives the eye nowhere to land.
- **Gold is an accent**: a chip, an icon, the capture button. Never a field.

## 2.1 The language, complete

**This section is the build manual.** The mockups are six screens and the app
has more than eighty. **A screen nobody drew is built from here, not guessed
at**, and a feature added next year needs no new mockup: it needs this. Every
number was measured off the approved PNGs at 1080 and 3x, so dp is px/3, or
taken from Material's own specification where the drawings are silent. D183.

**What this is.** Material 3 Expressive as Google ships it, plus one thin layer
of this app's own character. **Where Material has an answer, Material wins.**
The layer on top is only what a care notebook needs and Material does not
mention: the road, the section hues, the eyebrow, and the rules about what may
never be said.

### The five laws

1. **A screen leads with one thing.** One element at display size answering the
   question the person opened the screen to ask. Everything else recedes.
   Uniform weight pushes the sorting onto somebody already exhausted.
2. **Contain what belongs together, and separate what does not.** Google's own
   research measured people finding what they came for **up to four times
   faster** when related things were grouped and the groups separated. Grouping
   is done with air first and a container second.
3. **Say state in more than one channel.** Color plus words, or weight plus
   position. Never color alone, never size alone.
4. **The app counts and never concludes.** No thresholds, no ranges, no arrows,
   no color that changes with a value, no progress against a person's own
   diligence. Rule 2 and rule 13.
5. **Everything touchable responds**, from the motion tokens, with one press
   treatment app wide.

### Surfaces

- Canvas is `paper`. Groups are **flat tonal blocks** in `sand` at
  `Radius.cardLarge`. Depth is `paper` against `sand`, never elevation.
- **One saturated block per screen at most.** The section's own wash for the
  thing that belongs to that section, `goldWash` for a single raised fact,
  `alertWash` for an open incident or the emergency card.
- **White (`card`) is reserved.** The person's own paper, and the actions band
  at the foot of a form. Nothing else earns it.
- **The only shadow in the app is under the paper card**, because paper casts
  one. `Modifier.raisedCard` in the theme, absent in dark.

### Space: three gaps and no others

| Gap | Value | For |
|---|---|---|
| `Space.withinGroup` | **8dp** | Two things answering one question: a field and its supporting line, a label and its block |
| `Space.betweenGroups` | **24dp** | One question and the next. Material's own section value, and what `Page` puts between its items |
| `Space.betweenZones` | **32dp** | The part that asks and the part that acts. Spent once per screen |

Screen margin is `Space.screenHorizontal`, **16dp**, Material's compact margin.
A row carries `Space.rowVertical`, **13dp**, above and below its content, which
puts list rows on a **64dp** pitch. A block's own padding is `Space.ml`, 20dp.
**Uniform air is why a screen reads as one long thing**, D188.

### Type

`DESIGN.md` 5.1 is the ladder and `check_type_ladder.py` holds it. The jump from
a screen title to a row title is **2.3x**. Two text colors, `ink` and `ink2`; the
third level comes from size and weight, never from a paler ink, because `ink3`
is not text.

- **Display M** is the screen's one lead. **Display S** is a block's own title.
- **Row title** 17sp is the title line of a row.
- **Body L** is a sentence somebody reads; **body** is support; **body S** is an
  aside.
- **Support** 12sp is what a field wants, under the field. Material's supporting
  text size, and one step below any prose beside it. D189.
- **Eyebrow** 12sp tracked is the quiet label naming what follows: the section's
  `ink`, or `goldInk` on the canvas. **Capitals only where the words are the
  app's own and short.** A label somebody typed keeps its case and drops the
  tracking, and carries the natural words as its description.

**A label's weight and the air around it must agree.** A heading eight points
under a field says two different things at once, which is the defect the owner
named on the capture form.

### Color

- **`blue` is every action and only actions.** One filled action per group at
  most; the rest tonal.
- **`gold` is the trail, capture, and a single raised fact.** Never a field.
- **`alert` is the emergency card and the open state.** Never a measurement.
- **Section hues are identity, never state.** `hueFor(section)` gives base, ink
  and wash: base for shapes, ink for text, wash for the surface behind them.
- **A person's mark takes their own hue**, picked from their id, which carries
  no category because nothing sorts or filters by it. D186.

### Marks

Material Symbols Rounded, filled, through `Symbols`. **This app authors no
glyphs.** A section's mark sits in a **44dp squircle at a 14dp corner** in the
section's wash, tinted its ink. A mark beside a word that says the same thing is
not announced. Inline marks are 20dp; a mark on a block is Material's own 24dp.

### Controls

- Material's own: `Button` filled for the commit, `FilledTonalButton` for
  everything else, both at `Radius.button`, `Switch`, `ShortNavigationBar`,
  `SegmentedButton`, `ModalBottomSheet`.
- **No outlines**: a container says tappable more clearly and adds no edge.
- **Weight and commitment are separate.** A filled action is the one thing a
  group offers; the tick is only for something that writes.
- **Actions are sized to their labels.** A full width bar is the exception, not
  the default.

### Fields

- **The label is persistent and carries the meaning**, in the notched outline.
- **The hint is supporting text under the field**, never inside it: placeholders
  are read as content already filled in, and they vanish exactly when they are
  needed. NN/g, D189. One supporting line per field, never two.
- **A person before their number**, always, and the name asked as a name.
- **Fields sit on the canvas, not inside a block.** A field is already a
  container; a second one is two edges around one thing. `m3v4-4`.
- Groups of fields sit under an eyebrow, `FieldBlock`.

### Which component for which content

| The content is | Use |
|---|---|
| A fixed set of destinations | `ListRow` with a mark, in one `Block` |
| A long list somebody scans | `ListRow` in a `Block`, divided |
| Three or more lines somebody reads | `Block` per item |
| One person | `PersonRow`, or `PersonHero` for the one they call |
| A sequence in time that actually happened | `Road`. **Never for a filter**, D187 |
| A thread, a search result, a subject's entries | A list, with the route beside the name if it has one |
| One raised fact | `FactBlock` |
| Two or three views of one list | `Segments`. Not folds, D185 |
| The person's own paper | `PaperCard` |
| A question with two or three answers | `ChoiceRow` in one block |

**Not everything is a card, and making everything a card is why nothing stands
out.** Reusing the layout of the screen you arrived from is a deliberate choice,
never the default.

### What a screen owes

Rule 11's states: **empty, one, many, partially filled, long text, loading and
error**, every one of them built. Rule 15's hierarchy. Rule 16's press on
everything touchable. Rule 19's reader, maximum font and reduced motion, verified
on the phone. Nothing is behind a fold that a label and a scroll can carry, D185.

### Building a screen nobody drew

1. **Name the one thing** the person opened it to find. That leads, at display
   size, in a block if it belongs to a section.
2. **Group everything else** by the question it answers, each group under an
   eyebrow, `betweenGroups` apart.
3. **Pick each group's component from the table above**, by the shape of its
   content.
4. **Put the actions last**, sized to their labels, one filled at most, on the
   band if the screen is a form.
5. **Walk the states** from rule 11, then look at it on the phone at font scale
   2.0 and in dark. Rule 21.
6. **Log it**: rule 12's three places, at the moment of building.

## 3. The order, and it is not negotiable

Each step is finished before the next begins, because every step below reads
its defaults from the step above.

1. **The theme.** `MaterialExpressiveTheme` replaces `MaterialTheme`, with the
   expressive motion scheme, the color scheme, the shape scale and the type
   scale. Every component beneath it reads shape, color and motion from here.
2. **The shared surfaces**: the container, the action, the row, the field, the
   sheet, the navigation. These reach every screen at once.
3. **The screens**, one at a time, each rewritten against the foundation.

**A screen is rewritten or it is left alone.** Nothing is half converted. The
build never carries two design languages at once, which is the state that made
the previous attempt unusable.

## 4. What replaces what

The old interface is deleted as each piece is replaced. These are the objects
to remove, in the order step 2 removes them:

| Gone | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| the hairline-divided white monolith | tonal containers with air between items |
| the outlined pill button | filled and tonal Expressive buttons, with their own corner |
| the full width back footer on every section screen | the platform's own navigation |
| the sand accordion | nothing: the screens stop needing it. D185 |
| the chip, small title and gray sentence header | an editorial header that leads |
| the app's hand-drawn switch, chevron and controls | Material's, themed |

## 5. Rules that bind this work

- **2.** No medical or legal advice, no interpretation, no thresholds, no color
  coding by value. Nothing in the new language may imply a judgment.
- **11.** A screen ships with its empty, one, many, partially filled, long
  text, longest language, loading and error states, and RTL, or it is not
  built.
- **13.** Partial is a finished state. No progress meters on the person's own
  diligence.
- **14.** The bar is retroactive.
- **15.** Hierarchy before decoration.
- **16.** Everything the person touches responds, from the motion tokens.
- **19.** Accessibility is a gate. Verified with the reader on, font at
  maximum, reduced motion actually enabled, never by reading code.
- **21.** Look at it on the phone before closing anything.
- **24.** Version one ships English. RTL verified on every screen against a
  forced layout direction. The four catalogs stay complete.

## 6. Working notes

- **Measure the mockup in pixels before deciding anything looks close.** Three
  defects on 2026-08-16 were invisible to judgment and obvious to a
  measurement: the app was set in a different typeface than the drawing, the
  icon tile's corner was within two points of a circle, and the rows sat on a
  54dp pitch where the drawing sits on 64. **"It breathes" is a measurement
  once somebody takes it.** The captures are 1080 wide at 3x, so dp is px/3.

- **Read the last line of `tools/seed.sh` before trusting a screenshot.** It
  says "Restored." or it did not finish, and every capture after a failed seed
  is of an empty notebook.
- **Seed at font scale 1.0.** `seed.sh` taps by position and a raised font
  scale moves the restore screen's controls out from under it.
- **Read test counts, never the exit code.** A piped run reports the pipe's
  status: a run that failed seven tests exited zero. The counts are in
  `android/app/build/outputs/androidTest-results/connected/debug/TEST-*.xml`.
- **Do not compile while the instrumented suite runs.**
- Screenshots: `tools/screenshot.sh <name>`. Navigation for a walk is by nav
  bar position; `tools/walk.sh tap` matches the first node containing the word
  and the capture button is described "Add something to the notebook".
- Device helpers assume `ADB=/home/Kamsiob/Android/Sdk/platform-tools/adb`.
